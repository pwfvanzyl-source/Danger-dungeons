# Danger Dungeons - Game Description

## Overview
Danger dungeons is a dice-powered dungeon crawler, inspired by dice-a-million and dungeon clawler

## Target Audience
It is made for all ages, from age 7-adults. It will contain violence

## Art Style
It has the cartoony artstyle of dungeon clawler

## Gameplay Mechanics
You roll dices and play cards to kill enemies and clear out the fortresses 100 floors

## Story/Setting
The story plays of in a giant fortress, where you seek to vanquish an evil king

## Game Features:

floors:
Each floor has a battle and a shop.
Your HP resets every floor
Every x floors, There is a forge and an ally bell.
X is the difficulty: easy: 5, normal: 10, Hard: 15
Every 20 levels, there is a boss

Enemies and their bosses:

HP: how much damage an enemy or ally or hero can sustain before dying

Attack: How much damage they deal to the nearest enemy (unless stated otherwise)

Defend: create's a shield for only the user that must first be taken down before dealing permanent damage. all block is lost at the end of turn (unless stated otherwise)

Buff: Gives itself and / or His ally's (or enemies, depending if its your ally or enemy) a special effect, like + stenghth or + defense to all allies / enemies

Heal : Hero: Heals X (X being your roll or stated amount of Heal) off selected target
       Ally: Heals X (X being stated amount of heal in ally actions) off selected target
       Enemy: Heals X (X being stated amount of heal in enemies actions) off of most damaged enemy
